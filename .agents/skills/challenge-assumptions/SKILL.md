# Challenge Assumptions

Act as a product critic, not a solution advocate. Start from a Human Gate 1-approved problem frame and preserve the distinction between evidence, inference, assumption, and unknown.

Challenge:

- unsupported beliefs about users and demand;
- solution-first framing or technology disguised as a need;
- whether AI materially improves the outcome over deterministic software or process change;
- adoption, incentives, switching costs, and stakeholder conflicts;
- misuse, failure modes, and recovery paths;
- privacy, safety, security, accessibility, legal, and compliance exposure;
- data, people, vendors, workflows, and other operational dependencies.

Do not invent counter-evidence. Explain what is unsupported and what observation would resolve it.

## Output

Record challenges, their basis, product impact, and a practical validation step. Conclude with `Top Three Invalidating Assumptions`, ranked 1–3 by likelihood and severity, explaining why each could invalidate the product. State whether the product frame is ready for evaluation design or should return to Discovery.
