# Knowledge Wiki

This wiki holds durable, synthesized knowledge that is useful across product concepts. It is not a dumping ground for transcripts, raw research, intermediate reasoning, or complete run output.

## Contents

- [Product principles](product-principles.md)
- [Evaluation philosophy](evaluation-philosophy.md)

## Promotion rule

Knowledge moves through three distinct levels:

1. **Run:** execution-specific evidence, drafts, and outputs live in [`runs/`](../../runs/README.md).
2. **Product artifact:** after human review, a stable concept-specific source of truth is promoted to [`products/`](../../products/README.md) with provenance.
3. **Wiki knowledge:** only cross-product learning that has been synthesized and can be supported or explicitly labeled as a principle is added here, with links to its basis and a revisit condition where relevant.

Do not copy raw material between levels. Summarize it, retain provenance, and leave uncertainty visible.
